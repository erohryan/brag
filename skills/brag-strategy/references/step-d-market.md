# Step D: Light market framing

A few web searches to learn what language and angles resonate *right now* in the product's category — so the content feels current, not generic. This is **light framing, not a competitive analysis.** Do not build a feature-comparison matrix or a positioning teardown. The goal is a handful of usable cues.

## Prerequisite: the real product name

Market framing needs the product's real name and category (Step A). If either is ambiguous, confirm with the user before searching — searching for the wrong product produces misleading cues.

## What to search (a few passes, not many)

Use `WebSearch` for a small number of targeted queries, for example:

- `[category] trends 2026` — what's ascendant in this space.
- `[category] marketing angles` or `how [audience] talk about [category]` — the language customers and marketers use now.
- `[product name]` — how the product is described in the wild, if at all.
- One query on a current-trend hook relevant to a headline feature (e.g. "AI [category]" if that's the moment).

Stop once you have enough for a short cue list. Do not fetch and read dozens of pages — this is framing, not research.

## What to extract

Into `brag-marketing/market-notes.md`:

- **Current angles** — 3–6 themes/positions that are landing now (e.g. "speed as the headline", "AI-native", "anti-bloat / simplicity", "outcome over features").
- **Language that resonates** — words/phrases customers actually use; and tired phrases to avoid (reinforce `/brag`'s ban on generic SaaS language).
- **Timeliness hooks** — any moment worth riding for a specific feature.
- **Source note** — one line on where cues came from and the date.

## Restraint

- Light touch. A dozen bullet points is plenty.
- Framing informs *tone and messaging*, not the truth about the product. Never let a market trend override what the product actually does.
- **Offline / no web access:** skip gracefully. Write a one-line note in `market-notes.md` that framing was skipped and proceed — the plan can still be built from features and outcomes.

## Gate

`market-notes.md` exists with a short list of current angles and resonant language, or a one-line note that market framing was skipped and why.
